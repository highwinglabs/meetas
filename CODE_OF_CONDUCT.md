# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
experience, gender, nationality, age, disability, or any other characteristic.

## The short version

- Be respectful and constructive; assume good faith.
- Critique ideas, not people, and stay on topic in code and review.
- No harassment, discrimination, or unwelcome commentary.
- Use inclusive language.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers (see [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) for how to reach
us). We will review the report, take action to help ensure the reporter's safety, and
may remove content or access in line with the policy below.

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/0/code_of_conduct/)
(v2.0) as its underlying policy.
